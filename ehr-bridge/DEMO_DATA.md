## What is in a chart

- Contact details, a varied DOB, a fictional phone number, an `example.test` email address, and an example address.
- Visit notes with chief concern, history of present illness, review of systems, examination, assessment, documented plan, and follow-up.
- Medication names, doses, units, and recorded schedules; reported allergies with reaction descriptions.
- Blood pressure, heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature, oxygen saturation, height, weight, and case-specific laboratory or examination findings.
- Past medical, family, and social histories, department and encounter information, and scheduled follow-up.
- Two actual PDF attachments per patient: a Valley report and a Memorial visit summary. PDFs are stored as authenticated file bytes in the central and source-hospital databases. The ankle report is an invented narrative; it contains no diagnostic X-ray image.
- Both native demo formats, the general representation, the source doctor/hospital, historical dates, and earlier source versions.

## Show approval and conversion

One **pending request from Dr. Emily Carter at Memorial for Sarah's Valley records** is included. No transfer or patient approval is pre-completed.

1. Sign in as Sarah and review the request in **Access requests**.
2. Generate the OTP and share it with the Memorial doctor.
3. Sign in as Emily in another browser profile and redeem it in **Transfer requests**.
4. Inspect the copied records, report, source payload, and field-level history.

Patients can see their complete central chart. Hospitals initially see their own visits and require patient approval to copy outside records.

## Populate again

From the workspace root:

```powershell
# Add new seeded profiles without changing existing charts:
.\ehr-bridge\scripts\seed.ps1 -Patients 24

# Stop Polymed first, then replace its demo databases and save backups:
.\ehr-bridge\scripts\reset-demo.ps1 -Patients 8
.\ehr-bridge\run.ps1
```

Seed is idempotent and leaves existing charts/accounts intact. Reset is the way to obtain a fresh detailed dataset when an older starter dataset is already present. All three old database files are backed up and checked before replacement. Backup directories under `.data/backups/` include restoration instructions in `README.txt`.
